PRELIMINARY HEADER FILES: RAT-A-TAT CAT (ENSC 251)
==================================================

The game is split into 9 header files across three folders: Deck, Player
and GameManager. Each class has one job, and the classes are built from each
other. GameManager owns the players and the decks, each Player owns a hand and
a set of rules, and the decks are built from cards stored in a linked list.


DECK FOLDER: THE CARDS
----------------------

singularLinkedList.hpp
  - A template linked list (Node<T> and singularLinkedList<T>) from Jethro's
    labs. It stores the cards for the Deck and PlayHand classes.

card.hpp
  - Stores one playing card as a rank and a suit.
  - Adds a Joker rank to Lab 1's card, because the game uses Ace to 10 plus
    2 Jokers.
  - getPoints() gives a card's value: Joker = 0, Ace = 1, 2-10 = face value.
    Power cards have no value.
  - isFaceCard() tells whether the card is a Jack, Queen or King, which are
    the power cards.
  - Keeps the comparison and stream operators from Lab 1.

deck.hpp
  - A pile of cards, from Lab 2, stored in a linked list where the top card
    is the head.
  - genCards() builds the deck: Ace-10 of each suit plus 2 Jokers, and J/Q/K
    too when power cards are on.
  - Also has shuffle(), drawTopCard(), addCard() and peekCard().
  - setNumCard() is private, because a deck is only ever empty or full.

DeckManager.hpp
  - Holds two Deck objects: the face-down draw pile and the face-up discard
    pile.
  - Handles the set-up steps from the official rules: building and shuffling
    the deck, cutting it, and turning over the first discard. If that discard
    is a power card, it goes back in the deck and another card is turned over.
  - Hands cards to players from either pile, takes discards, and shows the
    discard pile.
  - When the draw pile runs out, it shuffles the discard pile into a new
    draw pile.


PLAYER FOLDER: THE PEOPLE (AND BOTS)
------------------------------------

PlayHand.hpp
  - Stores a player's 4 face-down cards in a linked list, at positions 0-3.
    The two outer cards are positions 0 and 3.
  - Can replace a card (handing back the old one), look at a card without
    changing it, and clear the hand at the end of a round.
  - Can show the hand hidden or face up.

player.hpp
  - Stores a player's name, their PlayHand, their GameRules object and their
    total score.
  - ruleCheck() passes a drawn card to the game rules.
  - scoreCheck() adds up the hand's points at the end of a round.
  - isOut() tells whether the player has reached the score limit.
  - Its input functions are chooseDrawSource(), chooseCardPosition() and
    askYesNo(). They are virtual so a Bot can answer them itself instead of
    asking a person.

Bot.hpp
  - A computer player (a planned extension) that inherits Player.
  - Answers the same questions using its own rules, and remembers the cards
    it has seen in its own hand.
  - The exact rules the bot follows are still to be decided.


GAMEMANAGER FOLDER: RUNNING THE GAME
------------------------------------

GameRules.hpp
  - Holds the rules for using a card. Each Player has its own GameRules
    object.
  - A card taken from the discard pile must replace one of the player's
    cards.
  - A card from the draw pile can replace a card, be discarded, or use its
    power.
  - The power cards are Peek, Swap and Draw 2 (proposed: Jack, Queen, King).
    Draw 2 repeats if you draw another Draw 2.
  - At the end of a round, any power cards in a hand are replaced from the
    draw pile.
  - explainCard() and explainRules() print help text. Power cards can be
    turned off for the minimum viable game.

GameManager.hpp
  - Runs the whole game and decides whose turn it is.
  - Holds 2-3 players as a dynamic array of Player pointers, so the same
    array can also hold Bots. Its destructor deletes them.
  - Owns the DeckManager.
  - Each round, it deals 4 cards to each player, lets players peek at their
    outer cards once, and runs turns clockwise until someone knocks. Every
    other player then gets one more turn, and the round is scored. The deal
    moves left after each round.
  - Before each turn it clears the screen and asks the next player to press
    Enter to confirm it's them, so no one sees another player's cards.
  - Proposed: a player who reaches 100 points is out, and the last player
    left wins.
